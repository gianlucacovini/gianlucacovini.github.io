## Publications

- **Enhancing Parameter Control Policies with State Information** (joint work with Denis Antipov and Carola Doerr)
  *Proceedings of the Foundations of Genetic Algorithms (FOGA 2025)*, Leiden, Netherlands, 2025.  
  arXiv:2507.08368.  

  This work studies dynamic parameter control strategies for randomized optimization algorithms, showing how state information can be exploited to improve theoretical and empirical performance guarantees, *in collaboration with Denis Antipov and Carola Doerr*.<br>
  → [Paper (PDF)]({{ '/assets/files/Publications/FOGA2025.pdf' | relative_url }})
